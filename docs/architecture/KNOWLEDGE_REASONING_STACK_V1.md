# MIZAN Knowledge + Verification + Legal Reasoning Stack v1

This bundle advances three layers together while preserving epistemic separation.

## A6 — Evidence Resolution (EXTEND / CONNECT)
Existing Evidence Resolution remains authoritative for Observation relationships. It outputs only OBSERVED, CORROBORATED, CONFLICTED, UNRESOLVED, REJECTED, REVIEW_REQUIRED.

## A12 — Knowledge Layer (NEW CAPABILITY)
Typed knowledge: ENTITY, EVENT, RELATION, TEMPORAL_CLAIM, CANDIDATE_FACT. Every claim carries case_id, Evidence Resolution id, observation ids, stable locators, citations, confidence, and verification requirement. Candidate Fact is not Accepted Fact.

## Verification Gate (NEW CAPABILITY)
Candidate Fact may be VERIFIED, REJECTED, or REVIEW_REQUIRED. Even VERIFIED does not automatically become Accepted Fact. Admission remains a later governed contract.

## A13 — Legal Reasoning Foundation (NEW CAPABILITY)
Issue-centric reasoning explicitly carries supporting, opposing, and unresolved knowledge. Cross-case reasoning fails closed unless a future explicit linking contract is used. v1 forbids authoritative conclusions and outcome prediction.

## Hard law
Observation → Evidence Resolution → Knowledge Interpretation → Candidate Fact → Verification. No direct promotion to Accepted Fact.
